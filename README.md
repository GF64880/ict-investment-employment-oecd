# ICT Investment and Employment in OECD Countries

**Thesis:** Bachelor's Degree in Digital Economics and Business  
**University:** University of Brescia (A.A. 2023/2024)  
**Supervisor:** Prof. Jacopo Bonan

## Overview
Panel data analysis of the causal impact of ICT investments on annual 
employment across 19 OECD countries and 4 economic sectors from 2000 to 2019.

## Methods
- Descriptive statistics (by country, year, economic activity)
- OLS regression: Level-Level and Log-Log models
- Entity fixed effects (Model 3)
- Entity + time fixed effects (Model 4)
- Entity + time + sector fixed effects (Model 5)
- Sector-specific submodels

## Key Findings
ICT investments have a positive and statistically significant effect on 
employment when controlling for all three dimensions. The sectoral context 
plays a crucial role in this relationship.
